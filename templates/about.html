Our Story

A Karachi Table, Made for Sharing

Since 1993, Gohar Restaurant has welcomed guests in Orangi Town with the flavors Karachi loves. What began as a neighborhood place to gather has grown into a restaurant for families and friends looking to share a generous meal and warm hospitality.

Our menu brings together the character of Pakistani cooking: sizzling BBQ, hearty karahi and handi, comforting curries, Chinese favorites, and something sweet or chilled to finish. Whether you visit for a quick bite or settle in around a platter, we want every meal to feel familiar, satisfying, and worth sharing.
